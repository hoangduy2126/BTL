About the project
AURA is a digital art exhibition platform built around a single vision: that art should be felt, not just seen. VRTX Studio developed a complete UI/UX design system — spanning desktop, tablet, and mobile — combining an immersive dark-mode gallery grid, artwork detail pages with cinematic full-bleed displays, artist biography panels, and a curated collection browsing experience. Every screen is designed to dissolve the boundary between viewer and artwork, using spatial depth, dramatic typography, and ambient colour to recreate the atmosphere of a world-class gallery.
